Touch safety

Community Guidelines

These guidelines describe behavior expected from people using Touch.

Respectful behavior

Use Touch in a way that respects other people, communities, identities, privacy, and boundaries.

No harassment or hate

Touch does not allow harassment, threats, bullying, targeted abuse, hateful conduct, or content that attacks people based on protected characteristics.

No sexual exploitation

Touch does not allow sexual exploitation, coercion, non-consensual intimate content, sextortion, trafficking, or sexual solicitation that violates safety or law.

No CSAE or CSAM

Touch prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Content or behavior that exploits, abuses, grooms, sextorts, traffics, or endangers a child is not allowed.

No spam or platform abuse

Users may not use Touch for spam, scams, impersonation, malicious links, automation abuse, or attempts to disrupt communities or app systems.

Reporting concerns

Users can report concerns through in-app reporting where available or contact ijroy037@gmail.com. Child safety concerns can also be sent to the child safety contact listed on the Child Safety Standards page.
