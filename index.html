What Touch is

A social space built around mood, expression, and community.

Touch is a React Native social app developed by IJ Roy. It is designed for people who want to express thoughts through mood-based posts, reels, anonymous groups, and community conversations.

The app focuses on lightweight discovery, safer participation, and clear reporting paths for content or behavior that violates community expectations.

Core experiences

Simple features for expressive social connection.

Mood reels

Short, expressive media experiences organized around the feeling or context behind a moment.

Anonymous groups

Group spaces where people can participate without making public identity the center of every interaction.

Post queues

Queue-based posting helps communities manage flow, visibility, and moderation expectations.

Community chat

Conversation spaces for community members to connect around shared interests and moods.

Safety standards

Touch prohibits exploitation, abuse, harassment, and unsafe behavior.

Touch maintains child safety standards, community guidelines, and legal pages so users can understand what is allowed, how concerns can be reported, and how account deletion requests are handled.
