User account requests

Account Deletion

Touch users can request deletion of their account by email. Touch does not claim automated deletion on this website.

How to request deletion

  1. Email ijroy037@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Touch Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the Google account email used for Touch so the account can be identified.

Review and processing

Deletion requests will be reviewed and processed. Additional verification may be requested if needed to confirm account ownership or prevent unauthorized deletion.

Contact

For account deletion questions, contact ijroy037@gmail.com.
